Safety analytics

Freight Operations Safety Assurance

An operational safety dashboard that monitors observation outcomes, shows where findings requiring action concentrate, and checks whether assurance activity covers the operation.

  • PlatformPower BI · DAX · Power Query
  • Original connectionAirtable REST API
  • DataPrivacy-safe mock values

Business question

Are safety observations producing reliable coverage and actionable findings?

Counting observations alone does not show whether records are complete, whether the assessed outcomes are compliant, or whether monitoring is distributed across the operation. The dashboard separates volume, validity, outcome, and coverage so safety teams can distinguish activity from assurance quality.

Approach

Connect observation records to outcome and coverage decisions

The original solution connected Power BI directly to an Airtable base through its REST API. Power Query prepared the observation records, while DAX measures calculated total and valid observations, outcome shares, findings requiring action, and operational coverage.

  • Headline cards distinguish all observations from records carrying a valid assessment outcome.
  • The outcome mix separates compliant, observation-only, follow-up, and action-required states.
  • Action findings rank the observation types with the highest non-compliant counts.
  • Coverage views compare observation volume by activity type and operational section.
