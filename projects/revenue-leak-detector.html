Revenue Leak Detector

A single-page merchant risk monitor that turns lifecycle and performance signals into an executive summary, root-cause view, and prioritized watchlist.

  • PlatformPower BI · DAX · Power Query
  • InterfaceNative and custom HTML visuals
  • DataSynthetic demonstration dataset

Business question

Which merchants require attention, and what signal put them at risk?

Revenue leakage can emerge at different points in the merchant lifecycle: stalled opportunities, activation delays, onboarding friction, declining GMV, or missed expansion. When those signals live in separate fields, commercial teams struggle to compare exposure and decide where to act first.

The dashboard centralizes the signals into one monitoring view, moving from portfolio-level indicators to the specific merchants driving the result.

Approach

From detection rules to an action-oriented watchlist

I defined rule-based flags for each leakage category, prepared the merchant table in Power Query, and used DAX to aggregate the selected population. Country, industry, and account-owner slicers support targeted investigation without leaving the page.

  • Executive cards summarize estimated commercial exposure, leak rate, merchant population, active merchants, and deal value.
  • A leak-type breakdown explains which operational signal contributes most to the combined exposure measure.
  • The merchant watchlist pairs every high-priority account with its amount, category, and plain-language reason.
  • A reset control restores the intended default filter state after exploration.

Metric design

A prioritization measure, not an accounting forecast

Portfolio-data disclosure: This project uses synthetic merchant data created for demonstration. Names, values, performance patterns, and results do not represent a real company.

The dashboard’s combined exposure measure uses different scenario inputs: deal value for pipeline delays, annualized recent GMV decline for active merchants, and estimated expansion value for missed opportunities. It is best interpreted as an estimated commercial exposure used for prioritization, not as recognized revenue or a financial forecast.

This distinction is documented so decision-makers can use the ranking without overstating the precision of the total.
