Operational analytics

Freight Rail Delay Analysis

A Power BI report that consolidates monthly delay records and shows when delay changed, where it concentrates, and which services and recorded causes deserve attention.

  • PlatformPower BI · Power Query · DAX
  • PreparationEleven monthly extracts consolidated
  • DataPrivacy-safe mock values

Business question

Where is operational delay accumulating, and what should be investigated first?

Monthly extracts made it difficult to see the full-year pattern or compare delay consistently across locations, recorded causes, services, and delay codes. The dashboard creates one analytical view so users can move quickly from the trend to the categories behind it.

This was my first Tamheer dashboard and became the foundation for how I structured later operational reports.

Approach

Consolidate first, then prioritize the exceptions

I cleaned and appended eleven monthly source extracts in Power Query, standardized the category fields, and created measures for total hours, peak month, disputed records, and service-level totals.

  • The monthly trend makes the July step-change immediately visible.
  • Location and cause views show where recorded delay concentrates.
  • A train ranking identifies the services with the highest accumulated delay minutes.
  • The delay-code split separates direct, indirect, and other recorded classifications.

Data privacy

Training context preserved without exposing operational information

Privacy disclosure: The original project was built during my Tamheer training at Saudi Railway Company using operational data. For this public portfolio version, service names, locations, categories, and numerical values were replaced or changed. The displayed figures are mock data and should not be interpreted as company performance.

The public version preserves the analytical structure and reporting approach while removing the link to the original records.
